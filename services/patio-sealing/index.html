Patio Resealing (Pavers & Concrete)

Sealing protects pavers and concrete from water, stains and Wisconsin freeze-thaw cycles, and it brings back the color that fades over time.

Get a free quote (608) 769-0278

What's included

  • Cleaning the patio before sealing
  • Applying sealer to paver or concrete surfaces
  • Walkways and other hardscapes (ask about yours)

Who it's for

  • Faded or stained paver patios
  • Concrete patios and walkways
  • Outdoor spaces you want to protect long-term

When to book

Sealing needs dry, mild weather, so it is done late spring through early fall.

We do patio sealing for homeowners and for commercial properties, all around West Salem, La Crosse, Onalaska, Holmen and the rest of the area we cover.
